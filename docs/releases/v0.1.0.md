Copycat is a Docker-based Linux and cybersecurity practice environment: SSH into a real Kali shell, break things, and reset with one command.

**Pre-release.** First tagged release of a working project, for demonstration and controlled local use.

### What it does
- 40 guided labs, 12 of them graded out of 100 with `copycat-check`.
- `copycat-trouble` breaks something real and grades the diagnosis.
- Two isolated machines (Kali and an Alpine target); no volumes, host mounts or Docker socket; SSH bound to loopback.

### Verified
- CI builds the images, starts the stack, and runs `copycat-selftest` (84 checks); the startup readiness measurement is in `docs/READINESS-EVIDENCE.md` (20 of 20 runs on one machine).

### Known limits
- Needs Docker; Windows launchers are PowerShell, others are shell scripts.
- The readiness evidence is one machine and one Docker version.
- Intended for learning on your own computer, not for exposure to a network.

The attached zip is the source at the tagged commit; checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`. See the [changelog](https://github.com/5K-bit/Copycat/blob/v0.1.0/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
